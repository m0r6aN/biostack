## Actioned In This Pass

**1. Tamoxifen ATLAS numeric bundle withdrawn** (`tamoxifen-efficacy-atlas-extended-001`).

Re-review found two defects the correction did not reach, both understating harm:

- The crude endometrial proportions (1.80% / 0.97%) were computed over the full randomization denominators,
  although the primary **excludes women with hysterectomy recorded at trial entry** from that analysis. About
  17-18% of the cohort. Corrected, the proportions are roughly 2.15% vs 1.19%.
- Those crude figures **understate the years-5-to-14 cumulative endometrial cancer risk by roughly 1.7-fold**
  (actual: 3.1% vs 1.6%).
- Crude proportions were printed beside log-rank rate ratios that do not generate them.

All numeric estimates are removed from the statement. Direction of effect and the mandatory benefit/risk
pairing are retained. The source quotes stay in `extractedEvidence` as an accurate record of what the cited
secondary summary says; they are simply no longer asserted. `evidenceTier` lowered Strong to Moderate,
because the figures that supported Strong are no longer asserted. Numbers may be restored **only** after the
ATLAS primary (PMID 23219286) is authorized and anchored.

**2. Creatine safety claim `evidenceTier` lowered Strong to Moderate**
(`creatine-safety-common-adverse-effects-001`).

The 2026-08-29 pass raised this to Strong on the stated ground that the 2025 pharmacovigilance analysis gave
systematic quantification **and independent corroboration**. The 2026-09-06 correction withdrew the
independence half of that ground but left the tier, so the claim asserted Strong beside its own flag saying
the concern was not resolved. The tier now matches the evidence actually claimed.

Both changes are lowerings. No `confidence`, `fieldAuthorityRequired`, `claimType`, `context`, `sourceRefs`,
or `extractedEvidence` value was changed in either packet.
