# Article Two: Creatine and Hair Loss

Status: internal editorial brief only. Not a public article, approved packet, or
route. Prepared 2026-09-05; source details and access limits are in [brief.md](brief.md).

## Search Intent and Position

Primary question: Does creatine cause hair loss?
Working headline: "Creatine and Hair Loss: What a 12-Week Trial Actually Measured"
Counter-programming: distinguish a measured outcome from a hormone-based inference,
then distinguish a null result from a lifetime guarantee. Neither scare copy nor
supplement-industry reassurance earns an exemption from evidence grading.

Candidate answer, pending independent editorial/source review:

> A 2025 placebo-controlled trial found no significant group-by-time interaction
> for its measured hormone or hair outcomes over 12 weeks in resistance-trained men.
> That is evidence about this study's population and duration, not proof that
> creatine can never affect hair loss in anyone.

Do not turn the trial protocol into dosing advice. Do not claim that a non-significant
test establishes equivalence, no possible effect, or safety in unstudied populations.

## Proposed Structure

1. Answer the question in two sentences, with population/duration in the answer itself.
2. "What was measured": hormone and hair endpoints, placebo comparison, 38 hormone
   participants versus 37 hair assessments. Reconcile inconsistent recruitment versus
   randomization descriptions before stating the randomized count.
3. "What it found": report the primary paper's actual tests and uncertainty, not
   an invented zero-risk estimate. Do not call p>0.05 proof of no effect.
4. "What it cannot settle": longer durations, other populations, genetic susceptibility,
   circulating versus scalp androgens, and the scope of the hair-assessment method.
5. "Reading the criticism": identify AHLA as commentary; disclose FotoFinder/TrichoScale
   as well as Trichogram. Verify COI from the paper's own disclosures, distinguish
   author relationships from direct study funding, and do not infer falsity from ties.
6. "Source ledger": primary RCT first, commentary labeled separately, checked date,
   population/endpoints, limits, corrections policy, and a reviewed dossier link only
   if that exact route/content is actually live at publication time.
