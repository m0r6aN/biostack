# BioStack launch readiness ledger

**Re-verified:** 2026-10-25 at 15:13 ET against `origin/main@2e5f4978` (PR #593 merged) and production `https://biostack.cc` / `https://api.biostack.cc`. Live checks were read-only GETs.

**Recommendation:** **NO-GO / HOLD**. A free public launch becomes **GO** once gates G1–G9 are done.

**Reason:** the code and CI are in good shape, and today's deploy of `2e5f4978` passed. Production is not. It still serves knowledge data from a legacy worker on an old image: 158 records, including the merged `Testolone`, against 157 on `main`. The site also has no readiness probes, no proven restore, no alerting and no automatic rollback, so an outage or data loss would go unseen and could not be recovered. Stripe and Keon do not block a free public launch (see "Not blocking a free public launch").

This is the single current launch ledger. History before 2026-10-25 is in git (`git log -- docs/launch-readiness-ledger.md`).

## Rules this ledger follows (owner rulings)

- **Clinton Morgan** is the sole owner, operator and decision maker. His sign-off is final, and only he changes the recommendation. There are no role-owners and no "owners approve, then a release owner decides" process.
- **No human-gated approval.** The two-reviewer AI council (`docs/operations/ai-council-review.md`) is the content and approval gate. Consensus passes. Disagreement escalates to Clint with distinct options. Where an older doc asks for "human approvers" or a "named approval policy", read "AI council review, then Clint's sign-off".
- **No legal review, ever.** Terms, privacy and consent-law items are **known risks**: listed below and never blocking (canon `docs/canon/biostack-protocol-intelligence-canon.md`, "Known risks").
- **No affiliate anything.** No affiliate, retailer or vendor links, labels, disclosures, codes, roles or settings. `frontend/src/lib/copyLint.ts` (`3c:commerce`) lints for these terms.
- **Who does each gate:**
  - **Code PR (us):** a repository change, merged by Clint.
  - **Clint/Codex (Azure):** Azure or GitHub settings, done by Clint or by Codex on his PC under his session.
  - **Clint:** a step only he can do, such as a live sign-in with his inbox or a sign-off.
